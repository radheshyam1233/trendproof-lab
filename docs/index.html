How the test works

Signal and execution. If yesterday's close is above its moving average, hold the asset. Otherwise, hold cash. Change the position at today's close and measure the next close-to-close return.

Fair comparison. Both start with 100 and pay the same fee per buy or sell, including the closing trade. The holdout starts in cash and uses earlier prices to warm up the signal.

What this can show. The sample is simulated to illustrate research mechanics. Changing settings after seeing the holdout makes it exploratory. Save a fresh period for the next genuine test.
